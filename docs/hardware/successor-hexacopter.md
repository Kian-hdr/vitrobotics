# The successor hexacopter

The original cleaning prototype was based on an existing quadcopter. The later Fusion archive preserves a six-rotor development branch, with a complete assembly and separate nozzle-mount and rotor-guard designs. This makes the mechanical progression inspectable beyond the early flight video.

![Native Fusion view of the successor hexacopter assembly](../../media/hexacopter-v2-cad.png)

*Captured from the existing `20021-000-01_frame-assembly_v2` design in Fusion on 11 September 2026. This is a CAD view, not a photograph of a manufactured or flying aircraft. Base-frame/reference attribution is still being reconciled before public release.*

## What is in the assembly

Native inspection shows six rotor positions, a central frame plate, branching guard structures and landing legs. The guard forms combine outer ring sections with internal ribs around each rotor position. A design timeline is present in the model.

The source resides in the EXLUMINA hub under `UAVs / Aeroshine / Hexacopter`. The same folder contains:

| Design | Displayed last-update date | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| `20021-000-01_frame-assembly` | 24 January 2025 | Earlier assembly file |
| `20021-000-01_frame-assembly_v2` | 17 July 2025 | The assembly opened for the view above |
| `Hexacopter_Nozzle_Mount_v2.2` | 17 July 2025 | A dedicated nozzle-mount design |
| `Hexacopter_Rotor_Guard_v1.0.0` | 16 July 2025 | A dedicated rotor-guard design |

The parent Aeroshine folder also contains revised arm clips and `Source_File_Hexacopter_Rotor_Guard_v1.1.0`. These file names and modification dates document a later CAD development branch. They are not manufacturing or flight dates, and do not establish a complete revision order.

## Relationship to the earlier work

The adapter and guard families show how the cleaning payload and protective structures were developed around the aircraft. The hexacopter branch brings those component concerns into another assembly layout. Kian reports multiple successor builds; the surviving model provides a concrete source to reconcile against those builds.

The archive also contains Tarot 680 frame-reference exports. The project's custom modifications must be distinguished from inherited or third-party geometry before assigning authorship or releasing editable files.

## Evidence still to connect

Original studies are needed to explain the loads, constraints and selection of the branching geometry. Installation photographs and build records are needed to match this design to a physical configuration. No payload capacity, endurance, structural margin or successful flight is inferred from the CAD image.
